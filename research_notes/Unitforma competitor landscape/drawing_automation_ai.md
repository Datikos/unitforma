# Drawing/CAD/BIM automation, AI renovation & AI plan-render tools: competitors and building blocks for Unitforma

Research date: 2026-11-08. Method: web search (WebSearch) only. Direct page fetches were blocked by the session's network policy (getfloorplan.com and arxiv.org failed DNS resolution), so the claims below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not full reads. Where the cited page is a vendor page, a sponsored article or an aggregator/directory, this is said. Relevance labels: **DIRECT** = sells to developers for new-build sales and overlaps Unitforma's product; **ADJACENT** = overlaps one automation step but targets another buyer; **SUPPLIER/PARTNER** = could be a component, API or channel for Unitforma; **TOOL** = general consumer or pro tool.

## 1. Floor-plan recognition and digitization: who extracts apartments, rooms and areas from PDF/DWG and exposes them for sales?

### Takeaway
Plenty of vendors can recognise rooms, walls and areas, from raster or PDF and sometimes DWG. They fall into three groups, none of which does Unitforma's job: (a) takeoff and estimating (Togal.AI, Kreo, Beam AI) for contractors; (b) plan-to-3D or editable-plan tools (Planner 5D, RoomSketcher, Getfloorplan, Higharc) that work on one dwelling per file; (c) capture and space-data firms (CubiCasa, Pupil, Archilogic, Archilyse) for existing buildings, appraisal or facility management. I found no vendor that takes a multi-storey developer drawing set (multi-unit floor plates, DXF layers or hatches, local-script labels, level markers) and automatically produces a unit inventory plus sales portal. The developer-facing interactive selectors (Planpoint, 3D Estate, Maverick Frame and similar) are mostly built by hand or as a service.

### Cited Findings

Walls are detected separately as linear takeoffs, and the output is meant to be reviewed and reclassified — [Togal help center](https://help.togal.ai/en/articles/15602901-ai-powered-area-and-wall-detection)
- Togal.AI claims 98% accuracy on floor plans against estimator baselines, citing a University of Kansas study. The study itself was not found, so this is vendor-reported and unverified — [Togal blog](https://www.togal.ai/blog/ai-blueprint-reading-accuracy)
- Togal.AI is OEM'd into other tools: Cyncly Measure "Blueprint AI, powered by Togal.AI" — [Cyncly](https://cyncly.com/products/measure-desktop/blueprint-ai); eTakeoff add-on (Oct 2024) — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241030940672/en/eTakeoff-Announces-Availability-of-Cutting-Edge-AI-Takeoff-Solution)
- Kreo 6.0 (Mar 2026, vendor blog): computer vision splits each uploaded PDF page into zones and auto-names pages by drawing type, i.e. document classification. One click on a room outlines it and labels name, type and area using legend/table context — [Kreo](https://kreo.net/news-2d-takeoff/agentic-computer-vision-for-construction-drawings)
- Kreo Auto Measure 2.0 detects room boundaries, walls, windows and doors using "computer vision and text processing". Kreo claims its "Caddie" assistant extracts room names and areas even from scanned PDFs — [Kreo](https://www.kreo.net/news-2d-takeoff/1-000-000-new-rooms-in-the-kreo-dataset); [Kreo flooring page](https://kreo.net/trades/flooring-estimating-software)
- Attentive.ai (Beam AI takeoff) raised a $30.5M Series B led by Insight Partners in Nov 2025, bringing total funding to $48M. ### Inferences
- The specific chain Unitforma automates (vector or scanned *developer* PDF/DXF set → doc classification → unit labels/areas incl. Georgian → unit polygons from hatches/zones → floors from level markers → scale from dimension chains → portal) had no single-vendor equivalent in any source found. Takeoff tools (Kreo, Togal) come closest technically, including page/sheet classification and room labels with area, but sell to estimators and do not output a sellable unit inventory.
- Single-dwelling converters (Planner 5D, RoomSketcher, Getfloorplan) require one unit/level per file and, in RoomSketcher's case, manual scale. Unitforma's handling of whole floor plates is a real differentiator.
- Getfloorplan is the closest *AI-native* competitor on the buyer-visual side. It targets developers with a 3D tour widget and is testing AI personalization, but it is small (~$500K seed per aggregators) and service-like (24h turnaround).
- Higharc is the strongest well-funded "plan → structured model → buyer configurator" analogue, but it targets US single-family production builders, not multi-unit apartment developers in emerging markets.
- Planner 5D's recognition API is a candidate supplier/fallback for raster single-unit plans. Togal and Kreo are plausible OEM/benchmark references.

### Gaps
- No source found for Swapp (AI construction documents). Search returned nothing on it.
- Could not read CubiCasa's or Archilogic's official API docs: fetches were blocked and search returned only aggregators.
- No independent accuracy benchmark for any commercial recognition product (Togal's 98% is vendor-reported).
- Whether any vendor handles Georgian (or any non-Latin script) labels on plans: no evidence found either way.
- Pupil's "digital twins from floor plans" product for new-build developers: not found. Evidence covers only scan-based existing-building work.
- Profitbase/Macro-style CIS "chessboard" platforms (likely relevant regionally): searches returned nothing usable.

## 2. BIM/IFC-to-web for sales: does anyone offer developer sales portals built from BIM?

### Takeaway
BIM-to-web *viewers* are commoditised and often free or open source (Speckle, That Open/web-ifc, APS Viewer, Dalux, BIMx Web Viewer), but none of them is a sales portal. The only BIM-based buyer-facing product found is GBuilder (Finland), which sells a BIM-based option/material configurator to developers such as YIT. It is a direct competitor on the "IFC → buyer experience" axis, though focused on post-sale customisation rather than automatic unit inventory. Real-time engines (Unreal/Twinmotion pixel streaming) power agency-built selectors.

### Cited Findings
- GBuilder: "BIM-based customer journey management software". Buyers compare materials and fixtures with live cost and confirm by built-in e-signature. "The whole system and also the 3D models are based on DWG or IFC files". Customer: YIT — [GBuilder 2020](https://gbuilder.com/resources/gbuilder-revolutionises-off-plan-homes-3d-visualisations); [GBuilder blog](https://www.gbuilder.com/blog/why-does-configuration-matter); [YIT story](https://gbuilder.com/customer-stories/yit); [Construction Management 2018](https://constructionmanagement.co.uk/finnish-firm-creates-bim-based-software-customise/). It also offers an interactive home selector for off-plan sales — [GBuilder resource](https://www.gbuilder.com/resources/market-and-sell-off-plan-homes-with-an-interactive-home-selector-and-high-definition-3d-models). Most sources are 2018–2022, so current status is unverified.
- Graphisoft BIMx: has a Web Viewer to embed a model on a company website "to promote it", plus private hyper-models for selected clients. Example: 80 Goodhall Street, London residential-led scheme — [Graphisoft BIMx tag](https://www.graphisoft.com/tag/bimx); [Central Innovation](https://centralinnovation.com/aec/visualization/bimx). - That Open Company: web-ifc (WASM IFC parser) is listed as MPL-2.0 and engine_components as MIT (aggregator mirrors, verify LICENSE files) — [ecosyste.ms web-ifc](https://awesome.ecosyste.ms/projects/github.com%2FThatOpen%2Fengine_web-ifc); [GitHub web-ifc](https://github.com/ifcjs/web-ifc). - Dalux: free BIM viewer for IFC/Revit, claimed to handle 1M+ objects — [AEC Magazine](https://aecmag.com/news/news-dalux-launches-free-cloud-bim-viewer/); [Architosh 2025-01](https://architosh.com/2025/01/tooltalk-looking-at-dalux-the-worlds-fastest-bim-model-viewer). ### Inferences
- "IFC → web 3D" is not defensible on its own (free viewers abound). What could be defensible is IFC **space ↔ sellable unit linking** plus status/price overlay, which none of the viewers provide and which GBuilder does only partially and manually.
- Recommended building blocks consistent with AGENTS.md licence rules: web-ifc (MPL-2.0) / That Open components (MIT), IfcOpenShell (LGPL) server-side, or the Speckle viewer. APS adds Autodesk dependency and per-translation cost.
- Unreal pixel-streamed selectors are a high-cost, agency-made premium tier that Unitforma's auto-generated massing undercuts on cost and time, not on visual fidelity.

### Gaps
- No source found on IrisVR Prospect's current status (it was reportedly acquired by The Wild in 2022 — unverified, not searched).
- GBuilder's 2025–2026 product, pricing and whether IFC import is automatic: not found.
- No case found of a developer sales portal built directly on Speckle, That Open or APS.

## 3. AI virtual staging / renovation / furnished plan renders: which plug into developer sales sites?

### Takeaway
AI staging/redesign of *photos* is a crowded, cheap commodity ($15–50/month; APIs at ~$0.20/image), aimed at agents. AI "2D plan → furnished 3D plan render" exists from Getfloorplan, Planner 5D B2B, FloorVis, Rendair and many small tools, but outputs are illustrative, not measured. True-scale drag-and-drop furnishing on a plan is old tech (RoomSketcher, Floorplanner, Planner 5D, Roomle embed). I found no tool combining (a) auto-generated unit plans from developer drawings, (b) a true-scale buyer playground and (c) AI furnished renders inside a developer's own portal. Collov, Decor8 and Planner 5D APIs are the most likely suppliers.

### Cited Findings
- Collov AI: virtual staging API. You get an API key and POST room photos; the enterprise flow is async jobs with webhook notifications — [Collov API](https://collov.ai/features/api-for-virtual-staging-services); [Collov enterprise blog, 2026-03-28](https://collov.ai/blog/enterprise-ai-virtual-staging-collov-ai-enterprise) (SUPPLIER)
- REimagineHome: API available per a 2026 HousingWire review, starting ~$19/month (was $14 in 2025). Staging uses furniture linked to real retail catalogues — [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/virtual-staging-companies-apps/); [aixploria](https://www.aixploria.com/en/?p=9136) (SUPPLIER)

### Inferences
- The AI furnished-render feature is not a moat by itself. The moat is generating it **from the auto-extracted, true-scale unit geometry** so renders match the sold unit, which photo-staging tools cannot do for off-plan stock.
- For Unitforma, photo-staging APIs (Collov/Decor8) are optional suppliers for render-to-render restyling. Furniture catalogues (Roomle) could feed the playground.
- Watch Getfloorplan and Planner 5D B2B as the most likely entrants into "AI-furnished plans for developers".

## 5. Academic and open-source state of the art on automatic apartment/room extraction: how defensible is Unitforma's automation?

### Takeaway
Raster floor-plan parsing of single dwellings is a mature research area with public datasets. CubiCasa5K (2019, 5k plans), FloorPlanCAD (CAD vector, 10k+), ArchCAD-400K (2025), Swiss Dwellings/MSD (multi-apartment, 45k apartments), ResPlan (2025, 17k vector plans) and Raster2Seq (SIGGRAPH 2026) reach ~89 room-F1 on CubiCasa5K. General VLMs (GPT-4o/Claude) still fall short of humans on spatial floor-plan reasoning. Defensibility therefore lies not in room segmentation itself but in the end-to-end, multi-unit, vector-first pipeline: DXF layers and hatches, Georgian labels, level markers, dimension-chain scaling, IFC linking, human-in-the-loop correction and the sales data model. Georgian OCR in particular is weakly supported by open tools.

### Cited Findings
- CubiCasa5K: 5,000 floor plans with 80+ object categories, split 4,200/400/400, multi-task CNN baseline (2019) — [arXiv 1904.01920](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.01920). Licence believed to be CC BY-NC 4.0, from memory and unverified; check before commercial use.
- Raster2Seq (Cornell, arXiv 2602.09016, SIGGRAPH 2026): rooms as labelled polygon sequences with an autoregressive decoder. SOTA on Structured3D, CubiCasa5K and Raster2Graph; Room F1 ~88.7 on CubiCasa5K per an aggregator — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.09016v1); [Emergent Mind](https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2602.09016)
- A 2025 MiT-UNet hybrid improves wall segmentation, pretraining on CubiCasa5K — [arXiv 2512.02413](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.02413v2). arXiv 2609.25615 (Sep 2026) is "Evidence-gated multimodal parsing and vectorization of architectural floor plans", compared with CubiCasa5K, Raster2Seq and MiT-UNet; results not verified — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.25615)
- FloorPlanCAD (2021): 10,000+ CAD floor plans; introduced panoptic symbol spotting on vector CAD primitives; public — [arXiv 2105.07147](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.07147)
- ArchCAD-400K (2025): 413,062 annotated chunks from 5,538 drawings (~26× FloorPlanCAD), 27 categories, residential only 14%. DPSS baseline code released; first dataset round opened 2025-10-16 — [arXiv 2503.22346](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.22346v3); [GitHub](https://github.com/Lulzx/ArchCAD); [NeurIPS 2025 slides](https://neurips.cc/media/neurips-2025/Slides/115808.pdf)
- Swiss Dwellings (Archilyse): v3.0.0 has 45,176 apartments. An earlier version had 42,207 apartments / 242,257 rooms in 3,093 buildings, with ~400k areas and ~670k openings — [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/7070952). MSD (ECCV 2024) is the first large-scale dataset with a significant share of multi-apartment floor plates — [arXiv 2407.10121](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10121v3); [mlanthology](https://mlanthology.org/eccv/2024/vanengelenburg2024eccv-msd)
- ResPlan (2025): 17,000 residential plans with vector geometry, room graphs and a 17-class taxonomy, in pixel and metric coordinates; public data and code. Limitation: single-storey units, no multi-floor linkage — [arXiv 2508.14006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14006v1); [Moonlight review](https://www.themoonlight.io/review/resplan-a-large-scale-vector-graph-dataset-of-17000-residential-floor-plans)
- VLMs on floor plans: GPT-4o and Claude-3.5 reach up to 0.96 success on navigation plans, with accuracy dropping on larger maps and label density mattering — [arXiv 2409.12842](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.12842v2). SVG decomposition helps GPT-4o's spatial reasoning on 75 plans — [arXiv 2511.03478](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.03478). Blueprint-Bench (photos → 2D plan) — [arXiv 2509.25229](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25229v1). A Jul 2026 write-up of Blueprint-Bench 2 says frontier models remain "significantly below human baseline" (secondary source) — [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/blueprint-bench-2-tests-whether-ai-models-can-actually-reason-about-3d-space-and-the-results-should-recalibrate-what-spatial-ai-startups-expect-from-frontier-apis/)
- Georgian OCR: Tesseract has official `kat`/`kat_old` traineddata, but distributions package it inconsistently. Community trainers report "passable, but not excellent" results. A Georgian handwriting-OCR paper says existing systems supporting Georgian have low accuracy. No quantitative printed-text benchmark found — [Alpine issue](https://gitlab.alpinelinux.org/alpine/aports/-/issues/11531); [tesseract-ocr group](https://groups.google.com/g/tesseract-ocr/c/R_-9cduyixc); [BJMC paper](https://www.bjmc.lu.lv/fileadmin/user_upload/lu_portal/projekti/bjmc/Contents/13_2_07_Chikashua.pdf)
- Commercial vendors build proprietary training sets as a moat: Kreo touts "1,000,000 new rooms in the Kreo dataset" — [Kreo](https://www.kreo.net/news-2d-takeoff/1-000-000-new-rooms-in-the-kreo-dataset); Getfloorplan claims 1M+ plans converted — [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/getfloorplan)

### Inferences
- Defensibility assessment:
  - **Low**: raster room segmentation of a single-unit plan (public SOTA models and datasets exist; Planner 5D/RoomSketcher already productise it).
  - **Medium**: vector CAD/PDF symbol and room parsing (datasets exist, but ArchCAD-400K is only 14% residential and nothing targets "sellable apartment" extraction on multi-unit floor plates).
  - **Higher**: Georgian/local-script label reading on plans, level-marker → floor inference, dimension-chain scaling, hatch/zone → unit polygons on DXF from ArchiCAD/AutoCAD conventions, IFC space ↔ unit linking, plus the correction UI and accumulated tenant-corrected data.
  The moat is mostly workflow plus data flywheel plus local market fit, not any single model.
- MSD/Swiss Dwellings is the most relevant public training/eval data for multi-apartment floor plates. Check licence before commercial use.
- Frontier VLMs are usable for assistive tasks (document classification, label reading) but not reliable for measured geometry. This supports Unitforma's deterministic vector-first approach with an LLM/VLM in the loop.
