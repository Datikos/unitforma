# Global interactive new-development sales platforms (apartment selectors, digital sales suites, 3D unit configurators)

Scope: Western Europe, UK/Ireland, Nordics, North America, Middle East/UAE, Australia/NZ, Asia. CIS/Turkey/CEE and pure CRMs / drawing-automation tools are excluded except where they overlap. Research date: 2026-11-08.

**Method caveat.** WebFetch could not resolve vendor hosts in this session (gbuilder.com, maverickframe.com and datadrivenaec.com all failed DNS), so every finding below comes from web-search result snippets of the cited pages, not from full-page reads. Treat figures as "as indexed by search". Most sources are vendor marketing or sponsored content; that is flagged where it matters.

---

## Q1. Which of the suggested candidates exist, and what do they actually do?

### Takeaway
About half of the suggested names are real and relevant. Spark, Zonda (Envision plus the Alpha Vision site maps), Anewgo, Higharc, Lasso, Juniper (now part of Spark), PropVR, Archistar, Archilogic, Roomle, Floorplanner, PureWeb/Vagon, The Wild/Prospect, Property Finder, Bayut, Reelly, PropertyGuru and Matterport are real, though several are not apartment selectors. Others (Spacio, Plaace, VIZZIO, Exploreal, Propstack, Rex "Nurture", Eyerevolution) exist but do something different. The most relevant vendors are ones the brief did not list: Planpoint (CA), Maverick Frame (multi-country studio), gBuilder (FI), Pixel HomeSelector (UK), Buildmedia Realspace (NZ), konii FlatyFind/REALITIQ (DE), LotVue (ECI, US), Avesdo (CA), Simplex 3D REALS (US), Digs (US) and Getfloorplan.

### Inferences
- Outside the CIS/CEE region, the "site plan → building → floor → apartment with live availability" selector is mainly sold as a **studio deliverable**: Maverick Frame, Buildmedia, Bayut Studios, PropVR, konii, Render Vision and The Digital Bunch.
- Only two clear self-serve SaaS products were found:
  - **Planpoint** (2D/3D stacking plans with a no-code editor).
  - **gBuilder** (BIM-based, but sales-led onboarding with Lite/Pro/Enterprise tiers).
- North American leaders (Spark, Avesdo, Lasso) are **CRM / transaction-first**. Visual selection is partnered (Spark–Planpoint) or bought in (Zonda–Alpha Vision).
- US single-family vendors (Zonda, Anewgo, LotVue, Higharc) are built around **lots + plan options**, not stacked apartments. - Planpoint: self-serve stacking plans.
- Spark/Avesdo: inventory + CRM + contracts.

  - CRMs: Spark, Avesdo, Lasso, Rex, Vitec, Propstack.
  - Portals: Property Finder, Bayut, PropertyGuru, ImmoScout24, FINN.
